    How the reveal works

    Easy starts at 8px blur. Normal starts at 16px. Hard starts near 26px. Every wrong guess lowers the blur and fills the reveal bar.

    Hints unlock automatically: creator after 2 misses, difficulty after 3, song after 4.

    Your Thumbnaille

    Correct guesses and average attempts are daily-only stats.

    Thumbnails: levelthumbs.prevter.me (real community thumbnails, loaded by level ID).If an artist removes a thumbnail, the card shows “Thumbnail unavailable”.

    How to play Thumbnaille

    Name the level behind the thumbnail.

    1. 1A real Geometry Dash level thumbnail is loaded from levelthumbs.prevter.me. It starts blurred.
    2. 2Type a level name and pick it from the list. You have six attempts.
    3. 3Every wrong guess reveals more of the image. Hints for creator, difficulty and song unlock as you miss.
    4. 4Daily is one shared image per date. Practice is endless and never changes your stats.

    Difficulty changes the starting blur only. It does not change the daily answer or the stat rules.
