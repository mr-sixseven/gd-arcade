How to play Connections

Group four levels that belong together.

  1. 1Select four levels and submit them. A correct group disappears and reveals its category and difficulty tier.
  2. 2You have four mistakes. A wrong group costs one mistake and the remaining levels are reshuffled.
  3. 3Find all four groups before the mistakes run out. Categories include song, creator, imagery, title style and real level attributes.
  4. 4Every board is checked for overlapping answers. Levels that could fit two visible groups are never placed together.

Daily uses one deterministic board per date. Practice generates a random compatible board.
