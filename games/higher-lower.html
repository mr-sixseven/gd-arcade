How to play Higher / Lower

One stat. Two levels. Three possible answers.

  1. 1The left level shows its value. The right level's value is hidden.
  2. 2Choose Higher, Lower or Tie. A tie is a real answer — some levels share the same star count.
  3. 3Daily is ten comparisons with a fixed mix of downloads, likes, stars and Demonlist positions. You need 8/10 to clear it.
  4. 4Practice never changes your daily score. It ends on your first wrong answer.

Demonlist note: a smaller position number is a better rank, so “Higher” means a better Demonlist position for that category.
