    How the excerpt grows

    Start with 1 second. Each wrong guess extends the clip: 2s → 3s → 5s → 8s → 12s. Six attempts total per song.

    The play button resumes; the replay button starts the current excerpt again from the beginning.

    Scoring & hints

    A first-guess solve is worth 1,000 pts. Every miss costs 150, and each revealed hint costs 75. Hints unlock automatically after a miss.

    How to play Songle

    Name the track from a growing audio excerpt.

    1. 1Press play to hear the current excerpt. The first clip is only one second long.
    2. 2Search by song title or artist, choose a suggestion, then press Guess. You have six attempts.
    3. 3Every miss makes the next excerpt longer and unlocks a hint: artist, title shape, then level usage.
    4. 4Daily is one shared track per date. Practice is a single random song. Endless chains solved songs until one gets away.

    Audio note: streams are supplied by Newgrounds. If your browser blocks playback, use the direct track link shown after the answer or open the Newgrounds audio page.

    Music rights belong to the artists. GD Arcade links to their official pages and never hosts audio files.
