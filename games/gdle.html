    How to play GD-le

    Find the daily level in six guesses.

    1. 1Type a level name and pick it from the list. You have six guesses.
    2. 2Each guess compares difficulty, creator, song, length, version, downloads, likes, stars and demon rank.
    3. 3Green means exact. Yellow means close. Grey is a miss. The arrow tells you whether the answer is higher or lower than your guess.
    4. 4On the final guess, the answer’s thumbnail appears as a last hint. After the round the full level card is revealed.
